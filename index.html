Everything your garden needs

One app for the whole growing season

Botanisht models your garden as faithfully as a spreadsheet — then turns that model into calm, timely, location-aware guidance.

Multi-zone gardens

Model outdoor beds, greenhouses, windowsills, grow-lights, terrariums, containers and raised beds — each with its own geometry, microclimate and soil conditions.

Rich plant profiles

Water, light, soil, temperature, humidity, fertilizer (NPK), pruning and propagation for every plant — plus companion & clash planting and a full pest & disease library with organic treatments.

Climate-aware advice

Botanisht knows your USDA hardiness zone, AHS heat zone and Köppen/Trewartha climate class — so recommendations fit your actual local conditions, not a generic average.

Wireless sensors

Connect Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi-Fi (mDNS) sensors — plus USB serial — for live soil moisture, temperature, humidity, CO₂ and light (PPFD) readings streamed straight into the app.

Hydroponic telemetry

DWC, NFT, ebb & flow, drip, aeroponics, wick or Kratky — Botanisht polls your sensor endpoints and shows live pH, TDS, temperature & humidity with OPTIMAL / CAUTION status.

Smart reminders

Watering, feeding and pruning schedules run in the background and surface as local notifications — no server, no account, no forgotten tomato plant.

Live weather

Uses your location to pull real-time weather and fold it into care timing — water before the heatwave, hold off when rain is coming.

Dashboards & charts

Trend your sensor data and growth with interactive charts and gauges, so problems show up as a colour shift long before they show up on the leaf.

Modeled, not guessed

Your garden, mapped zone by zone

Most apps treat a plant as a name in a list. Botanisht treats your garden as a real place — with sun exposure, wind, soil pH, drainage, aspect and elevation captured per zone.

  • ✓ Outdoor, greenhouse, indoor, hydroponic, container & raised-bed zones
  • ✓ Sun exposure, wind, slope, aspect and last soil-test tracking
  • ✓ Per-zone microclimate: average temp, humidity and conditions
  • ✓ Hardiness, heat and climate-zone classification auto-derived from location

From soil to solution

Real-time hydroponic monitoring

Point Botanisht at your sensor's HTTP endpoint and it polls every few seconds, saving every reading locally and colour-coding the result. Walk away and the background service keeps logging.

  • ✓ Supports DWC, NFT, ebb & flow, drip, aeroponics, wick & Kratky
  • ✓ Live pH & TDS/EC with OPTIMAL / CAUTION / OUT-OF-RANGE bands
  • ✓ Temperature, humidity and "last seen" freshness at a glance
  • ✓ Every reading persisted to a local Isar database for trend charts

Yours, truly

Open source and private by default

Botanisht stores your garden in a local database on your device. There is no account to create, no telemetry leaving your phone, and the full source is public so you can verify every claim.

  • ✓ Local-first: Drift + Isar, fully functional offline
  • ✓ No ads, no tracking, no subscription
  • ✓ MIT-licensed source you can build yourself
  • ✓ Dark & light themes and multi-language ready

import 'package:botanisht/botanisht.dart';

// Define a garden zone
final bed = Zone(
  type: ZoneType.raisedBed(
    specs: RaisedBedSpecs(
      widthMeters: 1.2,
      lengthMeters: 2.4,
      heightMeters: 0.3,
      material: RaisedBedMaterial.wood(
        woodType: 'cedar',
        treated: false,
      ),
      hasIrrigation: true,
    ),
  ),
  conditions: ZoneConditions(
    sunExposure: SunExposure.fullSun(hoursDirectSun: 7),
    soilPh: 6.4,
  ),
);

// Track live hydroponic readings
sensors.addEndpoint(SensorEndpoint(
  name: 'Reservoir A',
  url: 'http://192.168.4.1/telemetry',
  pollInterval: Duration(seconds: 30),
));
