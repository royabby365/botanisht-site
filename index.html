Everything your garden needs

One app for the whole growing season

Botanisht keeps your garden organized and your plants healthy — all on-device, with nothing leaving your phone.

Multi-zone tracking

Organize every plant across Indoor, Kitchen, Hydro and Diagnostics tabs. Each zone keeps its own conditions, so a windowsill herb and a garage hydro rack both get the care they need.

Plant care profiles

Per-plant water, light, soil, temperature, humidity, fertilizer (NPK), pruning and propagation guidance — plus companion and clash planting rules that warn you before enemies sit side by side.

Companion & clash alerts

Botanisht's on-device engine checks your layout and flags bad neighbors — like basil next to rue — and highlights good pairings. All computed locally, with no network call.

Privacy-Centric Location Sync

Grounded in your neighborhood. Just enter your garden's ZIP code to unlock localized weather and frost indicators — without handing over continuous background GPS. Botanisht watches your local environment profile for sudden shifts so you stay ahead of changing cultivation windows.

Local-First Environment Logs

Track every variable internally. Log soil metrics, moisture and hydroponic levels straight into your private on-device database. Whether it's windowsill herbs or a multi-rack system, Botanisht structures your manual pH, TDS and moisture readings into clean environment profiles. Wireless hardware sync arrives in V2.0.

The Diagnostics Dashboard

Catch problems early. A unified health matrix monitors every zone at once. When a crop shows a care deficit or trips a companion-planting clash, the Diagnostics view surface-highlights the warning instantly — keeping maintenance predictable and structured.

Universal Design Themes

Read it your way — switch between Cream, Evergreen and High-Contrast themes with strict readability scaling, so the app stays comfortable for every eye and every light.

Data portability

Your data stays yours. Everything lives in a local NoSQL store (Isar) with offline JSON export and import — back up, move, or quit anytime with zero accounts and zero lock-in.

Log it, don't lose it

Hydroponic environment logs

Record your reservoir's pH, TDS/EC, temperature and humidity by hand and let Botanisht structure each entry into a per-zone profile. No hardware required today — automated sensor sync is on the roadmap.

  • ✓ Track DWC, NFT, ebb & flow, drip, aeroponics, wick & Kratky systems
  • ✓ Manual pH & TDS/EC with OPTIMAL / CAUTION / OUT-OF-RANGE bands
  • ✓ Temperature, humidity and "last logged" freshness at a glance
  • ✓ Every reading persisted to a local Isar database for trend views
